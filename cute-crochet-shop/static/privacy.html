Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At CuteCrochet Shop, we collect personal information you voluntarily provide when registering an account, placing an order, or contacting support. This includes your name, email address, shipping address, and phone number.

2. Payment Security

All online payment transactions are processed securely through certified payment gateways (Razorpay). We do not store or process your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to fulfill your orders, send order status updates and invoices, provide customer support, and improve your shopping experience. We never sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please contact our support team at support@cutecrochet.shop.
